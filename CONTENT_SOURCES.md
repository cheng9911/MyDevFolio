Updated: 2026-11-12.

## Owner-provided material

The owner supplied a September 1, 2026 research-defense presentation and an explicit
list of 14 projects/subtopics with personal responsibilities. The biography,
education, research progression, six paper/manuscript entries, ten patent records,
and two embedded platform images are grounded in those materials.

Authorized read-only checks of the owner's project assignments, execution plans,
and debugging records corroborated the listed roles. Raw internal documents,
source URLs, account identifiers, contracts, partner personnel, schedules,
credentials and debugging dumps are not included in this repository.

- A project entry records participation, not principal-investigator status or completion.
- Scheduled milestones are not treated as completed work.
- Shared papers and patents are counted once, not per funding project.
- Medical-control papers are research explorations, not algorithms claimed to have
  been delivered in the engineering system or clinically validated.
- Generic medical-arm and heavy-load robot entries retain the limited participation
  scope supported by the owner's list, without invented technical responsibilities.
- Doctoral study is ongoing; 2027 is an expected graduation year. The slide's
  malformed undergraduate start year is avoided by stating graduation in 2021.
- Acceptance, review and patent-status labels are explicitly dated to September
  2026 unless a later public record is specifically cited. This is a personal
  scholarly catalogue, not a real-time legal-status service.

The Biomimetics paper was subsequently verified as published on September 2, 2026: Biomimetics 11(9), 622, DOI 10.3390/biomimetics11090622. The site uses that publisher record instead of the presentation’s earlier acceptance status.
